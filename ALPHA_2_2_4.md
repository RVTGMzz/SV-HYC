# v0.0.7-alpha.2.2.4 — Controller Focus + One Stage Per Day

- Fixes the roommate/conversation controller selection being overwritten by a stationary mouse cursor.
- Adds a controller fallback for custom Sudoku menus during transient TV-sequence state.
- Limits Puzzle Bond progression to one new Stage clear per in-game day; cleared Stages remain replayable.
- Daily Challenge stays separate, so the player can do one Daily Challenge plus one new Stage on the same day.
- The next new Stage is available the following in-game day; 18/18 therefore requires at least 18 in-game days.
- Fixes Spirit's Eve test aliases: `wild` maps to the real `free` mode, and `/lewis` or `/reset` are accepted when passed as the argument to `heyyourecursed_test_spiriteve`.
- Keeps the alpha.2.2.3 dialogue readability pass and ChaCha-like ghost hover/no-shadow presentation.
